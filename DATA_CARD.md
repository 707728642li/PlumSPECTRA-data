
# Dataset card

## Scope

The source ledger links 5,502 plums. The primary modelling release contains 4,853
fruit from 15 cultivars for nine texture endpoints; 4,843 have complete fruit weight,
SSC and pH. Spectra contain 228 bands spanning approximately 901–1701 nm.

## Measurement order

NIR spectrum → fruit weight → duplicate penetration tests → SSC → pH.

## Quality control

Technical exclusions were defined without model residuals. Replicate agreement,
acquisition diagnostics, multivariate texture checks and spectral diagnostics were
combined in an auditable ledger. The complete cultivar L611 was excluded from the
primary modelling cohort because its spectral acquisition quality failed the frozen
gate; retaining it would require new splits and a complete refit.

## Limitations

The dataset does not represent independent orchards, years or instruments. Thirteen
of 15 cultivars have one acquisition batch. Texture position-derived quantities use
archive position units where physical motor calibration was unavailable. Cultivar
codes for breeding selections are not botanical variety names.

## Access and license

Private review only. Public redistribution and reuse terms are pending author and
institutional review. The data contain no human-subject or personal information.
